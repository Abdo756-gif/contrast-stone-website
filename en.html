Materials

What we work with

Three core materials, each with its own strengths and place in the kitchen.

Quartz

An engineered material with high resistance to scratches and stains, and consistent color with no variation between pieces — practical for a kitchen used every day.

Marble

Natural veining means no two pieces are alike, giving a classic, luxurious feel suited to spaces that get a lot of attention.

Granite

One of the hardest natural stones available, heat- and scratch-resistant, and one of the longest-lasting choices for a working kitchen surface.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the site visit free?

The site visit costs 1,000 EGP, deducted from the contract value if we go ahead with the work.

How long does installation take?

About 15 to 20 days from the contract date.

Do you offer imported materials?

Yes, we supply most types of imported quartz.

Do you cover all of Egypt?

Yes, we cover the whole of Egypt. Outside Cairo, transport and installation fees are added depending on location.
